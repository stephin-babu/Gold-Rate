📖 Kerala Gold Knowledge & Open Source Transparency

Understand Kerala gold measurements, hallmarking, and open architecture

⚖️

What is 1 Pavan?

In Kerala, 1 Pavan (also known as sovereign) is standardly defined as 8 Grams of gold. Ornaments in Kerala are predominantly priced and weighed in Pavans.

🔍

BIS 916 Hallmark

916 indicates 22 Karat gold (91.6% purity). Always ensure ornaments carry the mandatory 6-character alphanumeric BIS HUID code for guaranteed purity verification.

💡

Making Charges & GST

Standard Kerala jewellery purchases include the base gold rate, plus Making Charges (usually 5% to 15%), followed by a 3% central GST tax applied on the total.

🌐

100% Open Source

Zero tracking, zero ads, open JSON schema. Easily fork, customize, or host free on GitHub Pages for local communities, shops, or personal financial tracking.
